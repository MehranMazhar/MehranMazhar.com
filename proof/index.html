What I can prove

Three pillars

Reliability Engineering

Refunds retry under a bounded cap, re-inquire with the bank instead of assuming failure, persist gateway state immediately, and lock on the credit, not the queue. The four gateways I integrated, into an abstraction I did not design, carry 98% of card volume — about 1.33M transactions in 2025.

Automation and Production Ownership

Refund turnaround dropped about 85%, a week to a day, air and hotel, finance approval retained. Mine since 2020.

Service and Component Delivery

Verticals from zero to live: two-airline interlining, an airfare limit-order engine, flexible search, gift cards. All five design specs behind the interlining ADRs are mine.
